TobinMUD

A classic text-based multiplayer world. Fight, level, cast, loot, and explore — free to play.

Connect

Play instantly with any telnet client:

telnet tobinmud.com 4000

Or use the native Windows client below for colors, sound, and a live HP/Mana/Move gauge out of the box.

Download the Windows Client

Download TobinMUD Client (.msi)

Installs to your own user account — no administrator rights needed, and the client silently keeps itself up to date.

  • Full color and sound (weapon/class-varied hit sounds, looping fight music)
  • Live HP / Mana / Move gauge bar
  • Command history (Up/Down arrow)
  • Simple, editable triggers — no scripting required
